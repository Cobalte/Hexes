# Sushi Neko Swipe Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** November 2, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the Sushi Neko Swipe app ("the App") stores and collects, and who it goes to. The App is made by Cloak & Quill Games ("we", "us").

## The short version

- Cloak & Quill Games does not collect, receive or store any of your information. None of it is sent to us.
- Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
- The App shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects and shares some information from your device so it can show and measure those ads. That information goes to Google, not to us.

## Information saved on your device

The App saves non-personal information on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes your game progress, high score, language choice and sound setting.

This information never leaves your device and is not collected by or sent to us. We don't even have a server set up to collect it.

Uninstalling the App deletes this information.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows a banner ad, provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure how they perform and prevent ad fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK built into the App automatically collects some information from your device and shares it with Google:

- **Approximate location:** your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
- **App activity:** interactions such as app launches, taps and ad views
- **App diagnostics:** app and ad performance information such as launch time and responsiveness
- **Device identifiers:** your device's advertising ID and app set ID

This information is sent directly from your device to Google, encrypted in transit. It is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. It is not sent to or saved by Cloak & Quill Games, and we cannot see it.

You can learn how Google uses this information at [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choices

- **Consent:** if you are in a region with privacy laws that require it, such as the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the App asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown.
- **Changing your consent:** in those regions, you can change your choice at any time with the **Privacy options** button in the App's settings menu.
- **Advertising ID:** you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings (on most Android devices: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**).
- **Stopping all collection:** uninstalling the App stops all collection.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Because younger players may still enjoy it, the App only requests ads rated as suitable for all ages (Google's "G" rating). We do not knowingly collect information from children, and as described above, we do not collect information from anyone.

## App stores

This policy covers only the App. The app store you download the App from (such as Google Play) may collect its own information under its own privacy policy.

## Links and other services

The App may link to websites or services that we don't run, such as this privacy policy. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices, so please review their policies.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top. Continuing to use the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
