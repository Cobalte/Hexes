# Sushi Neko Swipe Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 23, 2026

Cloak & Quill Games LLC ("we" or "us" or "our") respects the privacy of our users ("user" or "you"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information using Sushi Neko Swipe (the "Application"). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time and for any reason. We will notify you of significant changes to this policy or to how the Application handles your information with a notification inside the Application.

## COLLECTION OF YOUR INFORMATION

The Application contains a banner advertisement provided by Google AdMob. The Application collects and shares the following information: your approximate location (using your IP address), app activity, app diagnostics, advertising ID, app set ID and, if applicable, identifiers for accounts signed in on your device. This information is encrypted in transit and sent to Google and its advertising partners for the purposes of advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This information is not sent to Cloak & Quill Games LLC. For more information about how your information is handled by Google, visit https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

Additionally, the Application creates and stores Non-Personal Information on your device for the purpose of enhancing your experience with the Application (such as saving your game progress and your choice of displayed language). This information is not transmitted off your device. Cloak & Quill Games LLC does not keep any of your information for any reason.

## CONSENT

When required by law, the Application asks for your consent before personalized advertisements are shown. In those areas, you can change your choice at any time using the Privacy Options button in the Application's settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's settings. Uninstalling the Application stops all data collection by the Application.

Google and its partners may use your information to show you ads based on your activity in this and other apps and websites over time. The Application does not respond to 'Do Not Track' signals, but you can limit personalized ads with the options above.

The Application is not directed at children under 13.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland: Non-personalized advertisements, ad measurement, and fraud prevention may rely on legitimate interests instead of consent. Google may process your information outside your country, including in the United States. You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, and receive a copy of your information; Google handles these requests (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can also complain to your local data protection authority.
